⚠ Safety first. Unplug the appliance before removing covers. Mains boards can hold a lethal charge in the large capacitors after unplugging — treat the mains side as live until you have checked it with a meter and safely discharged it.

Scan a circuit board

Take the photo straight on, in good even light, with the whole board filling the frame. Avoid glare on shiny parts. Close-ups give better part identification.

To scan your own photos, add your proxy URL in ⚙︎ Settings. The demo works without it.
